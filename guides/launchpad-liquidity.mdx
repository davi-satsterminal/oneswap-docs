---
title: "Launchpad liquidity"
description: "How a launchpad liquidity position earns trading fees, and what each number on your position means."
---

Once a launchpad coin graduates, it trades on its own pool. You can add liquidity to that pool
with both CC (Amulet) and the coin, and earn a share of every trade's fee for as long as your
liquidity stays in.

## How you earn

- Every trade on the pool pays a fee. Part of it stays in the pool for liquidity providers.
- Your share of the pool decides your share of those fees.
- Fees stay inside the pool and add to your position, so they compound automatically. There is
  nothing to claim or harvest.
- You receive your fees when you remove liquidity, together with the rest of your position.

## Your position

Open the coin's page and find your position in the liquidity panel. Every amount is in CC,
valued at the pool's price at that moment.

| Number | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Position value** | What your position is worth now. |
| **Net return** | Position value, plus everything you removed, minus everything you added. |
| **Fees earned** | The trading fees your position has earned since your first deposit, including any paid out when you removed liquidity. |
| **Price change** | The rest of your net return: the effect of the coin's price moving against CC. |
| **Total deposited** | Everything you added, valued when you added it. |
| **Total withdrawn** | Everything you removed, valued when you removed it. |

Fees earned plus price change always equals net return.

## Reading your numbers

- **Fees earned** only grows while your liquidity is in the pool and people trade the coin.
- **Price change** can be positive or negative. A pool holds both tokens, so when the coin's price
  moves, your position ends up holding more of one token and less of the other.
- A **negative net return** with **positive fees earned** means the coin's price fell by more
  than your fees made up.
- **All LPs' fees (all time)**, under pool details, is the fee total for every provider in the
  pool, not just you.
